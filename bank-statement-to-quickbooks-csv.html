Bank statement to QuickBooks Online: the CSV format explained

When a bank feed is missing transactions (older periods, a closed account, a bank QuickBooks can't connect to), you can upload them yourself. QuickBooks Online is picky about the file. Here's the format it accepts and the mistakes that make an upload fail.

Where to upload

Transactions → Bank transactions → Upload transactions (the menu names move around between QuickBooks versions; look for Link account → Upload from file). QuickBooks accepts CSV, QFX, QBO and OFX. CSV is the most common for data you converted yourself.

Option A: the 3-column layout (simplest)

Date,Description,Amount
01/03/2026,STARBUCKS #2231,-6.45
01/04/2026,CLIENT PAYMENT - ACME LLC,1312.30
  • One signed Amount column: money out negative, money in positive.
  • Use one date format for the whole file. QuickBooks asks you to confirm it (e.g. MM/dd/yyyy) during the upload.
  • No currency symbols or thousands separators in amounts (1312.30, not $1,312.30).

Option B: the 4-column layout

Date,Description,Credit,Debit
01/03/2026,STARBUCKS #2231,,6.45
01/04/2026,CLIENT PAYMENT - ACME LLC,1312.30,

Separate money-in and money-out columns, both positive. During the upload you tell QuickBooks which column is which.

Errors that break the upload

  • Extra rows: titles, "Opening balance", "Total" or blank lines above or below the data. Keep one header row and the transactions only.
  • Mixed date formats: 1/3/26 next to 2026-01-04. Convert them all to one format.
  • Running balance column included: harmless if you map it to "don't import", but easy to mis-map as the amount.
  • Overlapping periods: uploading Jan 1–31 and then Jan 15–Feb 15 creates duplicates that you then have to exclude one by one. De-duplicate before uploading.
  • Credit card signs: on card statements a purchase increases the balance. Make sure purchases end up as money out.

Before you upload: verify the numbers

QuickBooks will happily import a misread amount. Check that opening balance + sum of amounts = closing balance for each statement before uploading. Our PDF-to-Excel guide has a one-line running-balance formula that pinpoints a bad row.
