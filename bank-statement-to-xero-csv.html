Import a PDF bank statement into Xero: the CSV format that works

Bank feeds only go back so far, and some banks don't connect to Xero at all. For those periods you import a statement file by hand. Xero doesn't read PDFs, so the statement has to become a CSV first. This is the layout Xero expects and the mistakes that get an import rejected.

Where to import

Open the bank account in Xero and choose Manage account → Import a statement. Xero takes CSV, OFX, QIF and QBO files. With a CSV you map the columns on the next screen; with OFX or QIF there's nothing to map.

The columns

Xero's statement template uses these headers. Only the two starred ones are required:

*Date,*Amount,Payee,Description,Reference,Check Number
03/01/2026,-6.45,STARBUCKS #2231,STARBUCKS #2231 SEATTLE WA,,
04/01/2026,1312.30,ACME LLC,CLIENT PAYMENT - ACME LLC,INV-1042,
  • Amount is one signed column: money out negative, money in positive.
  • Dates follow your organisation's region. A UK, Australian or New Zealand org reads 03/01/2026 as 3 January; a US org reads it as March 1. Check a few rows in the preview before you confirm.
  • Payee is what Xero shows during reconciliation and what bank rules match on, so a short, clean payee name pays off later.
  • No currency symbols or thousands separators: 1312.30, not $1,312.30.

Errors that block or spoil an import

  • Opening balance and total rows left in the file. Xero imports them as transactions. Keep only the header row and the transactions.
  • Two date formats in one file, often from pasting together statements from different banks.
  • Debit and credit in separate columns. Combine them into one signed amount before importing.
  • Overlapping statements. If January's statement ends on the 31st and February's starts on the 28th, remove the overlap before importing, or you'll be excluding duplicate lines during reconciliation.
  • Credit card statements where a purchase shows as a positive number. On a card account in Xero, purchases should be negative.

Check the numbers before Xero sees them

Once lines are in Xero, finding a misread amount means reconciling your way to the gap. It's faster to check the file first: opening balance + sum of amounts = closing balance for each statement.
