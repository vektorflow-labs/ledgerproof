LedgerProof vs DocuClipper

Both turn PDF bank statements into spreadsheets and accounting imports. They're built for different people. DocuClipper is a cloud service with a monthly plan. LedgerProof is a desktop app you pay for once, and your statements never leave your computer.

We make LedgerProof, so read this with that in mind. DocuClipper details come from its public pricing and product pages as of November 2026; check their site for current plans.

Choose DocuClipper if

  • You also need invoice and receipt extraction in the same tool.
  • Several people on a team share the work and need accounts and a shared history.
  • Most of your statements are scans, and you'd rather not set up an AI key.

Choose LedgerProof if

  • You'd rather not upload clients' bank statements anywhere.
  • Your volume comes in bursts (catch-up work, year-end) and a monthly plan sits idle the rest of the year.
  • You want a fixed cost: one payment, no page counting.
  • You import into tools that take OFX or QIF, such as Quicken, Wave, GnuCash or Banktivity.

The cost over a year

At about $29 a month, a year of DocuClipper's entry plan is roughly $350, with a page cap each month. LedgerProof costs $29 once with no cap. If you convert statements every week for a team, the cloud plan's extras may be worth it. If you convert in batches a few times a year, the one-time price is hard to beat.
