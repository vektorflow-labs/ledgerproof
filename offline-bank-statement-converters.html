How to choose

  • You use a Mac or Linux: LedgerProof or LedgerSprout. The other two are Windows only.
  • You need proof the numbers are right: pick a tool that checks rows against the statement's running balance, not only the totals. A totals check catches a missing row but can miss two errors that cancel out.
  • You import into Quicken, Wave or GnuCash: look for OFX or QIF export.
  • You get scanned paper statements: desktop tools read text PDFs best. LedgerProof can send scans to an AI reader you connect yourself (Google Gemini's free tier or a local Ollama model) and still checks the result against the balances.

Test before you pay

Whatever you choose, try it on two of your own statements, including your messiest bank. Then confirm the totals: opening balance plus all transactions should equal the closing balance to the cent. Our PDF-to-Excel guide has a one-line Excel formula that checks every row.
